========================================================================
             HOW TO ASSIGN DAILY ROUTES & STOPS TO DRIVERS
========================================================================

Follow these 3 simple steps to assign routes and stops to your drivers:

Step 1: Create or Confirm Your Routes
-------------------------------------
1. Log in to your Admin Panel and go to: Customers > Routes (or go directly to: /admin/routes).
2. Ensure you have created your routes, selected the active service days (e.g. Monday, Wednesday), and assigned a driver to each route.

Step 2: Access the Dispatch Dashboard
-------------------------------------
1. Go to the Dispatch Dashboard (or go directly to: /admin/dispatch-dashboard).
2. Under the "Routes Active Today" section, you will see the list of routes scheduled for today. Select route 

Step 3: Generate the Stops
--------------------------
1. Click the "Generate Stops" button located in the top-right corner of the dashboard page.
2. The system will automatically generate all scheduled stops for the assigned routes and push them directly to the drivers' mobile apps.

Done! Your drivers can now open their mobile app and see their daily routes and stop lists instantly.
